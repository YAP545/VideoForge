Frequently asked questions

What is a credit?

One credit lets you generate one 5-second clip. A 30-second video uses 6 clips = 6 credits.

Do credits expire?

No. Credits stay in your account until you use them.

What happens if a clip fails?

Credits are automatically refunded for any clip that fails to generate.

Can I try before buying?

Yes — you get 20 free credits when you sign up. No card required.

Which payment methods are accepted?

UPI, credit/debit cards, net banking and wallets via Razorpay (India).
