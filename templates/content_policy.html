VideoForge is a creative tool. These rules keep it safe for everyone.

What you cannot generate

  • Sexual content — nudity, explicit or suggestive content of any kind.
  • Graphic violence — gore, torture, beheading, mutilation.
  • Self-harm — content that promotes, depicts, or glorifies suicide or self-harm.
  • Real people without consent — videos that depict, defame, or impersonate real named individuals or public figures.
  • Illegal content — anything that violates applicable law, including child exploitation material, which is strictly forbidden and reported to authorities.
  • Misinformation — deliberately false content designed to deceive, including fake news videos.
  • Dangerous instructions — weapon making, drug synthesis, or similar harmful how-to content.

Enforcement

We run automated prompt moderation before every generation. Content that matches prohibited patterns is blocked immediately. Repeated violations result in account suspension.

If you see a generated video that violates this policy, use the Report button on the video page. Reports are reviewed by our team within 48 hours.

AI-generated content notice

All videos produced by VideoForge are AI-generated. A visible notice is shown on every video page. Do not misrepresent AI output as authentic human-recorded footage.

Appeal

If you believe a prompt was incorrectly blocked, email trust@videoforge.ai with your account email and a description of what you were trying to create.
